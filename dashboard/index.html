Method — how this radar is validated

Every value term in the model was tested keep-vs-drop on four held-out ways: two independent blind tests (seed-999 and seed-42, 17 protocols each), plus 5-fold cross-validation × 5 seeds, plus walk-forward (66 oldest protocols train, 29 newest test). The metric is median absolute log error of ln(market cap); smaller is better. Delta = drop − keep: positive means dropping the term made things worse, so the term earns its keep.

Uncertainty intervals — and how honest they are

Every board row shows a P25–P75 interval; every detail view adds P10–P90. The multipliers come from the v5 model’s leave-one-out residuals on the 128 comps (ratio of actual to predicted market cap): P10 ×0.0696, P25 ×0.2805, P50 ×1.0478, P75 ×3.6866, P90 ×8.4375. The interval is the implied value times the multiplier, computed at render time.

Coverage, measured on the 29 walk-forward test protocols: the P25–P75 interval covered 51.7% (15/29) — stated as a 50% interval, so it is slightly more honest than its label. The P10–P90 interval covered 72.4% (21/29) — stated as an 80% interval, so it is slightly less than its label. Both are stated as measured. Do not read these as “90% accurate”.

infinifi is the only protocol to list since the snapshot: the model implied about $8.3M for it, its real market cap was about $799k. A fresh launch can price at a small fraction of the typical mcap/TVL ratio (low float, thin early quote, down market). The model’s median prediction is a centre, not a promise.

Coverage — all 758 protocols accounted for

144 pre-token protocols are not ranked, and not silently: they are bridge, liquid-staking, exchange, versioned or wrapped products of tokens that already trade. Ranking them as “protocols awaiting a listing” would be factually wrong (the cases v5 avoided: WBTC, USDT0, CCIP, FBTC as “pre-token”).

The model

Fit at runtime from the snapshot’s own 128 comps (market cap > 0). 1. Global curve, OLS: ln(mcap) = 6.9646 + 0.5223·ln(TVL). 2. Pooled groups (Bridge, Staking, Yield, Lending, Dexs, everything else its own group; 26 groups): each group’s offset is its shrunk median curve-residual, w = n/(n+5); an unseen group falls back to the global median residual. 3. Ridge-LAD median regression (IRLS + ridge, λ=10, 40 rounds) on [1, ln(TVL), group one-hot, growth, log age, ln(chain count), 8 chain flags], continuous features standardised. 4. Final: ln(mcap) = 0.7·grouped + 0.3·ridge-LAD, exponentiated. The board separates protocols whose token already trades (128 comps, shown as live context) from the 198 still to come.

Farm plans are chain-specific: typical gas is about $8 on Ethereum, $0.01 on Solana, $0.50 on Base / Arbitrum / Optimism, $0.30 on BNB / Avalanche, $0.10 on Polygon, about $2 elsewhere. Each plan names amounts, costs and durations.

Data: DefiLlama public API, snapshot 2026-11-14 (758 protocols). A protocol is described as recording no token, never as having none. Implied value is this entry’s own claim. Nothing on this page fetches the network.
